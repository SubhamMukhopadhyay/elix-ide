# Elix IDE — Privacy Policy & Guarantees

## 🔒 Privacy Guarantees

1. **Local-First Storage:**
   - All source code, projects, custom categories, DSA practice records, and hackathon data are saved exclusively on your local machine.
   - Database location: `%APPDATA%/elix-database.json` (Windows) or `~/.elix-storage/`.

2. **Explicit AI Transmission:**
   - Code is **never** passively or silently transmitted to AI cloud APIs.
   - Only when you ask a question or request refactoring from the AI Agent is context sent.

3. **Telemetry:**
   - Telemetry is disabled by default.
   - No sensitive code, project names, or keystrokes are tracked.

4. **Zero Account Lock-in:**
   - Elix operates completely offline without mandatory sign-in or subscriptions.
